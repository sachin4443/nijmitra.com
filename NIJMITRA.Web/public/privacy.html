Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

NijMitra is built to keep your personal life private. This policy explains what we collect and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, optional email and profile information.
  • The records you choose to store (finance, documents, contacts, diary, reminders, chat).
  • Security data: sign-in history, device and browser type, IP address and audit logs, used to protect your account.

How we protect it

  • All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords and PINs are stored only as strong one-way hashes.
  • Password-vault items are encrypted on your device; our servers cannot read them.
  • Diary content, authenticator secrets and provider keys are encrypted at rest.
  • Every record is tied to your account and access is checked on the server for every request.
  • Administrators cannot view your vault, diary text or chat messages in admin screens. Any support access to your account is audited and you are notified.

Notifications

We send messages only through the channels you enable, except essential security messages (verification codes, password resets, important security changes).

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the delivery providers needed to send your notifications (email/SMS/push), or when required by law.

Your rights

You can view, edit and export all your data (Settings → Data & account) and request account deletion at any time.

Chat

Chat messages are protected in transit and visible only to conversation participants. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Screenshots cannot be technically prevented on the web.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Applies to the NijMitra website (nijmitra.com) and the NijMitra Android app.

NijMitra is a personal life-management service: you use it to keep your own finances, documents, passwords, contacts, Udhar (money lent and borrowed), diary, calendar, reminders and private chats in one place. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can delete it.

In short: we collect only what is needed to run your account and the features you use. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data to track you across other apps or websites. You can download or delete your data at any time.

2. Android app

The NijMitra Android app opens the same secure NijMitra service (nijmitra.com) inside the app, so this policy applies equally to it. The app:

  • uses the internet connection to reach NijMitra;
  • asks for permission to show notifications (Android 13 and later) — only so reminders and alerts can reach you; you can turn this off in Android settings;
  • can attach photos or files only when you pick them with Android's file or photo picker — it has no background access to your gallery, camera or storage;
  • contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the features you use and keep your records in sync across your devices.
  • To send reminders and alerts through the channels you enable (in-app, push, email, SMS).
  • To send essential security messages — verification codes, password resets, sign-in alerts and important account changes. These cannot be switched off because they protect your account.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and comply with the law.

We do not use your content to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

4. Sharing

Your information is shared only in these cases:

  • Delivery providers — when a message must be sent, the recipient's email address or mobile number and the message text are passed to the email, SMS or push service the platform uses to deliver it (for example an SMTP/email service, an Indian SMS gateway, and the browser or Android push service).
  • People you choose — chat messages go to the friends you message; a document share link gives access to whoever you send it to, until it expires or you revoke it.
  • Legal reasons — when required by law, court order or to protect users from fraud or harm.

5. Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords and PINs are stored only as strong one-way hashes.
  • Password-manager items are encrypted on your device; the server stores only encrypted data it cannot decrypt.
  • Diary text, two-step verification keys and provider keys are encrypted at rest.
  • Every request is checked so that a user can only open their own records and files.
  • Optional two-step verification, automatic sign-out after inactivity, and a full sign-in and activity history.
  • Administrators cannot see your passwords, diary text or chat messages. If support staff open your account to help you, this is recorded and you are notified.

Chat messages are encrypted in transit and visible only to the people in the conversation, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. Screenshots cannot be technically prevented — please share sensitive information carefully.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access & correction — view and edit your information any time in My Profile and Settings.
  • Download — Settings → Data & account → Download my data gives you a ZIP of all your records and files.
  • Delete individual items — any record, document, diary entry or chat message you sent can be deleted at any time.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Data & account → Request account deletion. Full instructions, including what to do if you cannot sign in: nijmitra.com/delete-account.html.
  • Notifications — choose which reminders and alerts you receive and on which channel (security messages excepted).

8. Children

NijMitra is a general-audience productivity service and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date above and notify signed-in users in the app before the changes take effect.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support.

Delete your NijMitra account and data

NijMitra (nijmitra.com and the NijMitra Android app) · अकाउंट और डेटा हटाने की प्रक्रिया

Option 1 — Delete it yourself (recommended)

Works the same in the Android app and in any web browser at nijmitra.com:

  1. Sign in to NijMitra with your mobile number and password.
  2. Open Settings → Data & account.
  3. Optional: tap Download my data to keep a copy of your records and files.
  4. Tap Request account deletion, enter your password and tap Delete my account to confirm.

Sign in to delete my account Go to Data & account

हिन्दी: NijMitra में लॉगिन करें → Settings → Data & account → “Request account deletion” दबाएं, पासवर्ड डालें और “Delete my account” से पुष्टि करें।

Option 2 — If you cannot sign in

Send an email to support with the subject “Account deletion request” and include the mobile number registered with NijMitra and your full name. To protect you, we will verify that the request comes from the account owner (for example with a code sent to your registered mobile number) before deleting anything. Requests are processed within 7 days.

Use Forgot password to regain access, then follow Option 1. If that is not possible, contact NijMitra support using the details in the footer of nijmitra.com with your registered mobile number; we verify ownership before deleting anything.

What happens after you ask

  • You are signed out of every device immediately and the account can no longer be used.
  • Your data is kept for a 30-day grace period in case the request was a mistake. To cancel during this time, contact support from your registered mobile number or email.
  • After the grace period, your account and data are permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, email, date of birth, address and photo.
  • All your records: expenses, income, budgets, recurring bills, documents and every uploaded file, password-manager vault and secure notes, product registers, custom tables, contacts, Udhar entries and payments, diary entries, calendar events and reminders.
  • Chats: your conversations and their messages and attachments, friend connections, requests and invitations.
  • Security data: two-step verification keys, recovery codes, sessions, trusted devices and push subscriptions.
  • Notifications, preferences and saved searches.

What is kept, and for how long

DataWhyHow long
Sign-in and activity logs (date and time, IP address, browser/device type, action name — never your content or passwords)Security, fraud prevention and handling disputesUp to 1 year from the date of each entry, then deleted automatically
Message-delivery logs (masked mobile/email, delivery status)Investigating delivery problemsUp to 1 year, then deleted automatically
Records of actions taken by NijMitra administratorsAccountability for staff accessKept as an internal audit record; contains no content from your account

Copies of data you shared with others (for example messages you sent are deleted, but a document someone already downloaded from your share link stays on their device) are outside our control.

Delete only some data

You don't have to delete your whole account: any expense, document, password entry, contact, Udhar entry, diary entry, event, reminder or chat message you sent can be deleted individually from inside the app at any time. Deleted files are removed from our storage within 30 days.
